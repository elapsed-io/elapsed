privacy

Effective November 15, 2026.

elapsed is a stopwatch for focused work, made by humanbn. It is built to know as little about you as possible: there are no ads, no analytics services and no tracking.

elapsed.io today

The timer has not opened yet. For now, elapsed.io shows a page saying it is coming soon, with this page and the terms. These pages run no scripts, set no cookies, store nothing on your device and count nothing.

Our host, Cloudflare, briefly processes standard request details, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver and protect the site. elapsed never stores them or uses them to identify anyone.

when the timer opens

The rest of this page describes the timer, once it opens at elapsed.io. You will use it without an account.

what stays on your device

The timer keeps what it needs in your browser, on your device. None of it is sent to us.

  • The session in progress: its start, pauses and end times, and the display modes it used. It is deleted when the session ends.
  • The session you just finished: kept in that browser tab only, so a reload can show its results again. It is deleted when the tab closes or a new session starts.
  • Your settings: your choices, such as the theme, font and display mode, once you change one.
  • Things you make: a font file you load and any custom CSS you write. They stay on your device and are never uploaded.
  • A copy of the site’s own files, so the timer works offline.

You can remove your settings and files at any time with “clear device data” in settings, under privacy and data. Your browser’s own controls for site data remove everything.

what we receive

  • Anonymous usage counts, on unless you switch them off in settings, under privacy and data. We count visits (a page load that is not a reload or a back-and-forward navigation) and sessions started, as daily totals and nothing else. A count carries no cookie, no identifier and nothing about you or your device, and nothing is stored on your device to make it.
  • Crash reports, only if you switch them on. A report holds the error message and where in the site’s code it happened, the version of the site, and your browser’s name and major version. Before it is sent, the parts of web addresses after a “?” or “#” are removed, addresses of other sites (such as browser extensions) are removed, and so is anything quoted in the error, since that could be something you typed. Reports are deleted after 14 days.

System notifications, if you switch them on, are shown by your device; nothing is sent to us for them.

what we never collect

What you are working on, your name or email address, your location, your activity in other tabs, or a fingerprint of your device. We set no cookies.

addresses

When crash reports are on, the timer’s server holds your IP address briefly in memory to allow at most five reports a minute from one address, then forgets it. It is never stored or logged.

where it lives

Usage counts and crash reports are stored in a database on Cloudflare, reached through the site’s own address and kept apart from everything else. Counts are kept as daily totals; crash reports for 14 days.

children

elapsed collects nothing that identifies a person, so it has no age limit.

changes

We will update this page before accounts arrive, since accounts will need more information, and whenever what elapsed collects changes. The date at the top changes with it.
